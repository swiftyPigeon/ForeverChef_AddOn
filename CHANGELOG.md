# Changelog

## 0.6.1

First public release.

- Every Cooking recipe in WoW: Forever (133) on ingredient tooltips: green when
  known, red when not, with how to learn it.
- Recipe window with recipe counters and a Zone column ("Multiple Zones" when a
  recipe can be learned in several). Click headers to sort and group; they
  stack, e.g. zones inside each Known / Not known group.
- Detail pop-up per recipe: ingredients, required skill, where to learn it, and
  the food's effect.
- Map waypoints (and TomTom arrows) to vendors, trainers and quest givers for
  as many recipes as you like; tracked recipes are highlighted, and each waypoint
  clears once its recipe is learned (the map pin then moves to the closest
  remaining one), or with the Reset All Waypoints button.
- Faction-aware sources; positions cross-checked between sources.
- Records recipe vendors, drops, quest rewards and NPC positions while you play,
  with `/fchef export` to share them.
- Pop-up messages above action bar 1 instead of chat lines.
